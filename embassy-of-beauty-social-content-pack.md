## 2. Ad strategy — three campaigns

### Campaign A — Prospecting: "The Cult of P50" (60% of budget)
- **Objective:** Sales (Advantage+ or manual: Lookalike of purchasers + interest stacks: Biologique Recherche, Augustinus Bader, La Mer, Dr. Barbara Sturm, SkinCeuticals, facialists)
- **Geography:** UK-wide
- **Why:** P50 has enormous latent demand (your search revenue proves it). The angle isn't "buy a toner" — it's *initiation into a cult product*. £85 entry price is the cheapest door into a £235-AOV relationship.
- **Offer:** no discounting needed (and BR likely restricts it). Instead: **free P50 sample-size or deluxe sample with first order** — you already stock £0 sample SKUs of P50/P50V/P50W, so this is operationally free.

### Campaign B — Retargeting: "Finish the Ritual" (25% of budget)
- **Objective:** Sales · Audiences: site visitors 30d, add-to-cart 14d, IG engagers 90d, Klaviyo list upload
- **Angle:** social proof + expertise. These people know the brand; they need a nudge and a reason to buy from *you* (official flagship, free expert advice, samples with every order, fast UK shipping).
- **Format:** carousel of bestsellers with star ratings/review pull-quotes; testimonial Reels.

### Campaign C — Local: Kensington Clinic Facials (15% of budget)
- **Objective:** Leads/Bookings · Radius: 10–15km around 3 Kensington Church Street + affluent London postcodes
- **Why:** clinic clients are your highest-LTV customers (they buy the prescription afterwards). A booked facial is worth far more than its ticket price.
- **Creative:** behind-the-scenes of the signature BR facial, the skin analysis moment, the calm of the treatment room. CTA: "Book your skin consultation."

**Budget guidance:** start £3–5k/month total. At your £235 AOV and 57% repeat rate, a blended CPA up to ~£60–70 on first purchase is still strongly profitable on LTV. Scale what hits.

## 5. Measurement & housekeeping (do these before spending)

1. **Connect Meta Ads to Windsor.ai** — your Windsor account currently only has Google Ads connected. Without Meta connected you can't see Instagram ad ROAS alongside Shopify revenue. (I can generate the authorization link when you're ready.)
2. **UTM everything:** `utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=p50-prospecting` etc. Right now 63% of your orders have *no* referrer attribution — tighten this so you can trust the social number as it grows.
3. **Conversions API / Pixel:** confirm the Meta pixel + CAPI are firing on the Shopify store (Shopify's native Facebook & Instagram app handles both).
4. **Klaviyo loop:** sync paid-social leads into a Klaviyo welcome flow; upload your customer list to Meta as a seed for Lookalikes and as an exclusion for prospecting.
5. **Brand compliance:** as the official BR flagship you're in the strongest position, but Biologique Recherche is famously strict about how the brand is advertised — confirm with your BR contact what's permitted in paid creative (especially product imagery and any pricing/offer messaging) before launch.
6. **Inventory check:** Lotion P50 Corps is at 0 stock — don't feature it until replenished.
